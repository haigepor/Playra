# Playra 首页和追番设计稿

两张图共用画面比例、字体层级、作品设定和导航。320dp 宽、160% 字号是设计目标，实际排版以 Flutter 渲染为准。

## 首页

Use case: ui-mockup. Deliver one polished, flat, front-facing Android phone screen for Playra, a Chinese-language anime streaming app. A single full-bleed portrait artboard around 320:700 proportions, edge to edge. Design for a 320dp phone with enlarged text (160% system font), comfortable legibility, naturally stacked labels, usable hit areas. Near-black charcoal canvas (#101115), pale warm-white Chinese sans typography, muted lavender (#bba9ee) only on selection or progress. Professional, content-led streaming interface. Original cinematic hand-painted anime key art, confident compositions with distinct silhouettes, fine grain, grounded lighting. No phone frame, presentation board, comparison labels, decorative slogans, explanatory paragraphs or watermark. Bottom navigation has exactly four evenly spaced icon + text items: 发现、日历、追番、我的.
首页 / Discover. Design one coherent screen with the visual hierarchy of an established streaming app. Top safe area and a short masthead, a restrained Playra wordmark on the left and simple search and history icons on the right. One large edge-to-edge widescreen feature still: in 夜航线 a silver-haired young navigator in an ochre oilskin stands on the right of a midnight harbour, reflected violet signal lights, cinematic deep indigo sky. Compose the left as quiet water and put the clear Chinese title “夜航线” near the lower left; very discreet carousel position marks. Below, the section “继续观看” has a compact horizontal shelf of widescreen film stills, visible two-up. Give “夜航线” and “森林来信” separate images, and label the current episode simply “第3集” and “第6集”; a thin lavender playback progress trace at the foot of each still. Next “热门动画” introduces a row of distinct vertical illustrated covers: 夜航线 (indigo coastal night), 森林来信 (sage woodland courier), 赤砂行者 (small figure under towering russet canyon cliffs). Use the limited physical width well: allow the third item to peek if necessary. Put titles in real typographic slots. Bottom nav active 发现. Make the artwork tell the story, with restrained controls and clear reading order.

追番 / Following. A calm, practical personal anime shelf. In the header, “追番” is a strong title on the left, “管理” a quiet, readable text action aligned on the right. Gallery layout: exactly two equal columns of large 3:4 portrait posters, then the third poster at the start of the next row. The empty fourth slot remains background. First title “夜航线”: silver-haired navigator in an ochre oilskin and harbour lights, indigo and violet. Second “森林来信”: a dark-haired courier carrying letters through a sun-dappled, jade-green forest. Third “赤砂行者”: lone desert traveller among vermilion cliffs, wind and sand. Each cover is a separate original composition. Under each image put the title, then a concise line in readable light text for personal progress and a secondary line for the latest released episode. Exact pairs in order: “看到第3集” and “更新至12集”; “看到第6集” and “更新至8集”; “尚未观看” and “更新至4集”. Distinguish personal progress from released episodes with consistent placement and tonal hierarchy. Leave sufficient line height and space on each card for large Chinese characters; emphasis through artwork scale and typography. No card shells or purple pills. Bottom nav active 追番; reserve bottom inset, content may scroll behind the safe viewport.
